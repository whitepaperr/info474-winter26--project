Rising Tuition, Rising Debt
This is our INFO 474 final project: interactive article using P5.js.

College is often described as an investment, but the price of that investment has changed a lot. In this interactive article, we look at U.S. tuition trends and total student loan debt over time using NCES and FRED data.

Team: Ariel, An, Tony

Tuition over time
Public and private 4-year tuition both rise over time. We also plot the average to show the overall trend. Hover to see exact values by year.
Average tuition vs Total student loan debt
Here, we compare the average tuition trend with total U.S. student loan debt. Note the different units on the left vs right axis.
Year Comparison
To summarize the change, we compare the first year to the last year using an index (start year = 100). This makes the growth easier to compare across measures.
Which grew faster? (index gap)
This chart shows the difference between debt growth and average tuition growth each year (index-based). Values above zero mean debt grew faster than tuition.
YoY Change Heatmap
Darker cells mean larger year-over-year changes. This helps spot periods with especially large shifts. Hover a year to see the exact YoY values.
